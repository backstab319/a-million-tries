Can you land it?

The engine can't hover: at its lowest throttle it still pushes harder than the booster weighs, so you light it at the last moment and reach zero speed at the deck. You get two lights: one to slow down high up (the entry burn, three engines), one to land (one engine). Light or stop the engine by holding the command through its half-second start. Land under 3 m/s, under 1 m/s sideways, leaning less than 8°, legs on the 52 m deck. Too much heat, air load or swinging across the airflow and it breaks up.
