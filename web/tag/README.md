# Tag vs my AI

The green robot chases, the yellow blob runs. Pick a side and play against any of the AIs from
the videos: four robot generations and four blob generations that taught themselves tag by
taking turns, over millions of rounds.

- **Same game for both of you.** The arenas, the movement and the ten decisions a second are the
  real game the AIs trained in, ported to JavaScript and checked against the original: eight AI vs
  AI rounds replay to within half a millimetre and end the same way, at the same tenth of a second.
- **You see what your character sees.** Obstacles cast shadows; the other player only shows while
  it's in your line of sight (a dashed ring marks where you last saw it). The AIs play by the same
  rule.
- **The AIs are real.** Their trained brains (27 numbers in: lasers, what they can see, the time
  left; two layers of 256 neurons; out comes a move) run right here in your browser.
- These 64 arenas are ones no AI ever trained in. Its creator's score against each AI (10 rounds,
  episode 8) is shown next to yours.

**Controls:** arrows or WASD (on a phone: drag). Esc to change opponent.

From the YouTube channel **A Million Tries**: https://www.youtube.com/@amilliontries
Models: https://huggingface.co/amilliontries
